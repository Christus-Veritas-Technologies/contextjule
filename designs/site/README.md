# site

`landing.html` is the marketing page as one standalone file. Open it directly;
nothing is fetched at runtime.

## The hero

The hero band is her stage, not a picture of her. A fixed sky gradient, three
stepped pixel clouds, a hill silhouette and a grass field with two tuft rows,
all generated by the same sprite engine that draws her. She walks the full width
of the band, stops, turns to face the visitor and waves, walks back, waves
again, and loops. About 22 seconds end to end.

The headline, body copy and buy button sit on a layer above her with pointer
events only on the interactive parts, so she passes behind the text freely.

The loop is driven by an interval reading the wall clock, not by
requestAnimationFrame alone, because rAF is throttled to nothing in a
background tab and would strand her mid-step. Both paths read the same clock,
so a sparse tick still lands on the right pose.

## Sections below the hero

Four bands, each a different daylight colour, so scrolling reads as time
passing rather than as one dark page:

    what it is           warm parchment, white cards, hard tan shadows
    what you see         dusk purple, the four load states side by side
    what it gives you    sky blue, the four rituals as cream rows
    price                grass green, the CTA repeated at full size

## The CTA

One button, twice on the page: `Buy Now` at `$4.99` with `$9.99` struck
through, and a static plate under it reading when the launch price ends. The
date is hard-coded copy, not a live countdown. If you want a real countdown,
that is a behaviour change, not a style change.
